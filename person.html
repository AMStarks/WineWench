Meet Alana

Wine writer, storyteller and Southern Highlands local. Pull up a chair.

Alana Kaye

Alana Kaye came to wine writing by way of theatre, food and books. The Wine Wench grew out of a wish to talk about the people who make wine and the pleasure of drinking it, in language everyone can join in with.

Based in the Southern Highlands of New South Wales, Alana champions Australian producers. A McLaren Vale grenache, Adelaide Hills pinot grigio, Margaret River rosé or Tasmanian sparkling can all find a place at her table. Rosé rarely has to wait for an invitation.

Thirsty Hungry

Thirsty Hungry is Alana’s food-and-wine video series for nights in. Each episode starts with a bottle and something to eat, whether that means cooking dinner or picking up a favourite local takeaway.
